Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At Ancient Heritage, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information while you explore our digital archives.

section 1

Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data to provide a seamless browsing experience. This includes account details created during registration and standard analytical data to monitor platform performance.

  • Personal credentials such as username and email address provided during sign-up.

  • Anonymous interaction metadata used exclusively for technical optimizations.

section 2

How We Use Data

Your data is strictly utilized to maintain the platform infrastructure. We implement a zero-commercialization standard, guaranteeing that your information is never shared or sold to third-party advertisers.

  • Authenticating institutional research credentials and securing private metadata silos.

  • Enhancing search functionalities and personalizing map interactions within the platform.

Data Security

Protecting identity, honoring scholarship.

section 3

Security Standards

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols to guard your data against unauthorized access. Our digital database relies on secure architectural boundaries to protect user accounts.

User profiles and academic submissions are encrypted to prevent illegal security breaches or identity exposure.

Any attempt to bypass encryption metrics or harvest metadata without institutional verification will lead to immediate profile suspension.

section 4

Cookies & Storage

Our system uses essential identifiers to remember your preferred viewing adjustments. These tokens store technical state metrics without mapping cross-site historical footprints.

Preferences

Saves display configurations such as dark mode and selected map coordinates.

Session Control

Maintains persistent administrative authentication during archival file queries.

section 5

Your Digital Rights

Registered members maintain complete ownership and control over their platform records. You have the explicit right to inspect, modify, or permanently delete your account file structure.
To request total account erasure from the Ancient Heritage Collective servers, contact the digital curatorial desk.

section 6

Policy Modifications

We may adapt this privacy documentation to comply with updated global legal regulations or technical infrastructure upgrades. Your ongoing site interaction implicitly confirms your agreement to these parameters.
